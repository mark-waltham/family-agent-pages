Privacy policy

Family Agent. Last updated October 24, 2026.

Family Agent is a private tool used by a single household. This policy explains what Google data it reads, what it does with that data, and who can see it.

Who uses it

Only the two parents in the household use Family Agent. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect information directly from children.

Google data it accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): messages in one family inbox. These are mostly schedule emails forwarded from schools, sports clubs and service providers.
  • Google Calendar: events on the calendars owned by the family’s Google account.

It requests only these two permissions. It cannot send email.

How the data is used

  • To find dates, times and places in forwarded emails and suggest calendar events.
  • To answer the parents’ questions about the family schedule and to prepare a weekly summary.
  • To check for scheduling conflicts.

Suggested calendar changes are made only after a parent approves them. The data is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not sold.

Where the data goes

  • The household’s own computer. Family Agent runs on a computer in the family’s home. Working copies, run logs and sign-in tokens are kept there, protected by an encrypted disk and restricted file permissions. They are kept only as long as needed to run and troubleshoot the tool.
  • Anthropic. To pull dates and details out of an email or answer a question, the text of the relevant message or events is sent to Anthropic’s API, a service the household pays for under Anthropic’s terms for business users.
  • Discord. Summaries and suggested changes are posted in a private Discord channel that only the two parents can read.
  • Google Calendar. Approved events are saved to the family’s calendars, which the household shares with its own family members.

Email and calendar content is not published or stored anywhere public. No one else is given access to it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Family Agent’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • You can revoke Family Agent’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deleting the stored sign-in token on the home computer also ends its access.
  • To ask about or request deletion of any data, use the contact address below.

Changes

If the way Family Agent uses Google data changes, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.
