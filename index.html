Family Agent

A private scheduling helper for one household. Last updated October 24, 2026.

Family Agent is a small program that one family runs on its own computer at home. It helps two parents keep track of children’s school, sports and appointment schedules. It is not a public product, it has no sign-up, and no one outside the household uses it.

What it does with Google data

  • Reads the messages in one Gmail inbox (read-only) that the parents forward schedule emails to, such as practice times and game changes.
  • Reads and adds events on the Google Calendars owned by the same Google account, so the family’s schedule stays in one place.
  • Posts a weekly summary and any suggested calendar changes to a private chat channel that only the two parents can see. Suggested changes are added only after a parent approves them.

The full details are in the privacy policy. The terms of service describe how the tool may be used.
